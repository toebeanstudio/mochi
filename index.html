Questions

Is it safe? What happens to my data?

Everything stays on your Mac. There's no account, and the app has no analytics or tracking. If you use the AI features, your messages go straight from your Mac to the AI provider you picked, with your own key. The optional browser extension (coming soon to the Chrome Web Store) only talks to the Mochi app on your Mac. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Everything in Free works without one. To let her talk and help with pages, add a key from Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT), or use Ollama, which runs on your Mac for free.

What does Pro unlock?

The AI brain (chat, memory, help with the page or app you're on, using your own Claude, OpenAI or Ollama), Pro wellbeing (focus sessions, breathing, stretches, your own reminders, mood check-ins and a weekly recap, wind-down), unlimited limits, all 11 furs in 3D and illustrated, mix-and-match accessories plus seasonal outfits, all 6 themes, and future accessories. It's was $14.99 now $9.99 once, for your Mac.

How many Macs can I use it on?

One Mac per licence. If you get a new Mac, move Pro to it from the app: open Mochi Pro, then choose Move Pro to another Mac. Free works on as many Macs as you like.

I lost my licence key. Can I get it back?

Yes. Enter the email you bought with on the Forgot your key? page and we'll email your key to that address again. Still stuck? Email hello@toebeanstudio.app with your Paddle receipt.

Will she mess with my windows?

Only if you ask her to. Calm sits with you and moves only with a reason. Playful adds a little fun, like stalking your cursor or bringing you a toy, and never touches your windows. Gremlin pranks every 10 to 20 minutes: she nudges and shrinks windows (always undoable), knocks things off, and hides, then ambushes you. Calm, Playful or Gremlin. You choose.

Will she slow down my Mac?

No. At rest she uses about 1 to 2% CPU. She only animates fully while she's doing something.
